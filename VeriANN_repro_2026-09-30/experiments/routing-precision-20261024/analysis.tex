Table~\ref{tab:routing_precision} isolates routing precision on identical
HNSW graphs and uses the same Merkle organization and progressive result
certification. Exact routing authenticates all vectors required to replay
unquantized-distance expansion decisions; vectors shared by routing and
result certification are transmitted once.
At matched Recall@10 of 95.00\%, SQ4 routing reduces VO size by
74.19\% and 72.76\% on SIFT and DEEP,
respectively, and reduces verification time by
58.71\% and 60.46\%.
On SIFT, SQ4 requires a larger budget and discovers more candidates at matched
recall, yet its smaller per-candidate routing disclosures and refinement-only
raw authentication substantially reduce communication and verification costs.
On DEEP, SQ4 reaches the matched recall with a smaller budget.
These measurements support verification-decoupled traversal on the evaluated
workloads; they do not imply that quantized routing always requires fewer
expansions or achieves higher recall. Calibration and timing use the same
20 query IDs, each repeated three times for timing.
